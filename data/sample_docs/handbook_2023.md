# Leave Policy
Full-time employees at Meridian Labs receive 20 days paid leave per calendar year. Leave must be approved by the department lead at least two weeks in advance. Unused leave balances up to 5 days may carry over to the subsequent calendar year.

# Remote Work Guidelines
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week. All staff must be physically present in the laboratory or office on core collaboration days: Tuesday and Thursday. Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

# Expense Allowance
Daily meal allowance during authorized business travel or late office shifts is strictly capped at Rs 2,000 per person. Original receipts must be submitted via the finance portal within 14 business days for reimbursement.

# Probation Period
New full-time hires are subject to a mandatory probation period of 6 months from their official start date. A formal performance review is conducted at the conclusion of month 5 before employment confirmation.
