# Leave Policy
Full-time employees at Meridian Labs receive 20 days paid leave per calendar year. Leave must be approved by the department lead at least two weeks in advance. Unused leave balances up to 5 days may carry over to the subsequent calendar year.

# Remote Work Guidelines
Under the updated hybrid framework, employees are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week. All staff must be physically present in the laboratory or office on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

# Expense Allowance
Daily meal allowance during authorized business travel has been increased and is capped at Rs 3,500 per person. Digital itemized receipts must be uploaded via the finance portal within 30 calendar days for reimbursement.

# Probation Period
New full-time hires are subject to a shortened probation period of 3 months from their official start date. A mid-point check-in occurs at week 6, followed by formal confirmation at the end of month 3.
