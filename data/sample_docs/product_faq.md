# Overview of Lumen Gateway
Lumen Gateway is Meridian Labs' flagship low-latency API proxy and routing engine designed for distributed AI inference pipelines.

# Pricing Tiers
Lumen Gateway is offered under two commercial subscription tiers:
- Starter Tier: Priced at $499 per month. Designed for high-growth startups and pilot workloads.
- Enterprise Tier: Priced at $2,499 per month. Includes dedicated edge clusters, custom routing plugins, and enterprise compliance reporting.

# Rate Limits and Throughput
- Starter Tier rate limit: Capped at 500 requests per second across all upstream endpoints.
- Enterprise Tier rate limit: Scaled to 10,000 requests per second with burst capacity up to 15,000 requests per second.

# High Availability and SLA Uptime
- Starter Tier SLA: Guarantees 99.9% monthly service uptime.
- Enterprise Tier SLA: Guarantees 99.99% monthly service uptime. Enterprise accounts feature an incident response SLA of under 15 minutes for critical severity outages.
